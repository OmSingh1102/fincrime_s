New onboarding cases as a CSV in the reconciliation log's layout, one row per case. Each row becomes a case at L1 Data Confirming with its World-Check hit and goes through the same steps as the file's cases: the AI screening rules run at once, and the cases they clear (auto-verified) are read by the AI Agent{% if not agent_ready %} — which needs LLM_FOUNDRY_KEY, so here they wait for an analyst{% endif %}, which verifies them when every check passes. Everything else goes to the analysts.

With real ID images: put the customers' photos or scans in a ZIP, each named after its case: KYC-2026-5001_passport.jpg, …_passport_back.jpg, …_secondary.jpg, …_poa.jpg (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each). Each one is checked, turned upright, saved without its EXIF data and put in its place on the case, and the AI Agent reads the images themselves{% if ocr_ready %}, with Tesseract's OCR of the passport's MRZ beside its read{% endif %}. A row may then leave the document columns empty: its case waits ("Awaiting Documents") until its passport and proof of address are in, from the ZIP or with Upload images on the case. The CSV and the ZIP together may be up to 100 MB.
