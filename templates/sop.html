SOP rules

The parameters the verification checks take from the SOP. Every check reads them when it runs: the proof-of-address checks on the Data Confirm dialog, the AI Agent's checks and the SLA clocks all follow a change at once, with no code change and no restart. When one changes, the open cases the AI Agent decided and nobody has touched since are checked again on its stored read (no new look at the images): one waiting for a new proof of address comes back and verifies if the one it holds now passes, and one with a person has its reasons brought up to date. Closed cases stay closed.
