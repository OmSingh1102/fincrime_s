Model evaluation

The two models of the Screening cases flow, measured on the IDs uploaded in this console as they are read and decided; the page follows every upload and every alert outcome. An upload has no answer key, so the reading model is measured by what the code's checks find on its read, and the deciding model against the analysts: each alert outcome an analyst gives is the answer its suggestion is scored on.
